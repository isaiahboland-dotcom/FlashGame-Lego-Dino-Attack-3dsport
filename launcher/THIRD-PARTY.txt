Programs this package uses besides the project's own:

- FFDec 26.4.0 (JPEXS Free Flash Decompiler, GPL-3.0), included unchanged in
  ffdec_26.4.0/ with its license.txt. Source and releases:
  https://github.com/jindrapetrik/jpexs-decompiler
- makerom (Project_CTR, https://github.com/3DSGuy/Project_CTR) and bannertool
  (https://github.com/diasurgical/bannertool), built from the pinned source
  revisions in the release workflow, for the .cia. Their licences are in
  those repositories.

Not included, and needed on your computer: Python 3 with Pillow and NumPy
(MIT-CMU / BSD), Java, FFmpeg, and devkitPro (devkitARM, libctru, citro2d,
citro3d). Install them from their own sites.
